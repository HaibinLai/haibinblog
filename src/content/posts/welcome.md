This is the first page of a new version of the blog. The purpose is not to make writing feel like software engineering. It is to make the archive durable, reviewable, and easy to carry forward.

## Why static

The public writing should be simple: Markdown in a repository, rendered into fast pages at build time. That makes a post a file rather than a fragile database row.

## Equations still belong here

Research notes need mathematics without ceremony:

$$
2^{28} = 268435456.
$$

The long-term system will keep interactive features separate from the writing itself.
